About

Hi, I'm Junpaul. I'm a product owner who started out as a developer, so I feel most at home between the people who use a product and the people who build it.

I earned my IT degree at Bukidnon State University in 2017, after an internship at Yahshua Systech. In 2018 I joined Wela School Systems as a web developer and grew with the team for almost seven years, moving from web developer to senior developer to product owner. Most of what I've built is school management software that schools across the Philippines use every day. Along the way I learned to take messy requests from schools and business teams and turn them into clear user stories a developer can pick up and ship.

Having written the code myself helps a lot. I can talk honestly with engineers about trade-offs and explain them to stakeholders in plain language. I keep the customer in mind throughout, so the team spends its time on what actually helps them. I've also earned certifications in product owner stakeholder management and in cybersecurity incident management.

Today I'm the product owner at Livro Systems Inc. in Cagayan de Oro City, where I lead the team building a school management system on Frappe/ERPNext.

I still love building things on my own time. Basa helps young learners practice reading aloud in English, Tagalog and Bisaya, Paws is a budgeting app with a cat that hands out money tips, GanttFlow brings Gantt chart planning into ERPNext, and Pickit is a browser game made with Phaser.

    Web app · works offline

    Reading practice for early-grade learners. Teachers build leveled lessons, from words to phrases to sentences to a short story, read aloud in English, Tagalog or Bisaya. Students read into the microphone, each word is marked right or wrong, and those who pass move up a level. It installs as an app and keeps working without signal, so rural schools can use it and sync scores later.
